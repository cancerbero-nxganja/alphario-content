## Legal Check — Why This Is Compliant

- **Format:** Opinion/commentary/satire — AI models expressing factual positions on public topics
- **Crypto content:** Analysis only, no buy/sell recommendations. Exchange affiliates disclosed. Disclaimer: "Contenido educativo. No es consejo financiero."
- **Voice synthesis:** Using ElevenLabs for fictional characters (AI personas), not to impersonate real humans
- **FTC:** All affiliate relationships disclosed in video description
- **RISK:** LOW. Closest risk is crypto content — mitigated by consistent disclaimers and "analysis not advice" framing already established in all eval-approved content.
